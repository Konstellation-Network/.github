# Security policy

Konstellation is pre-launch software. We take reports seriously at every stage.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report vulnerabilities **privately**, through GitHub's private
vulnerability reporting on the affected repository:

- The chain (`konstellationd`):
  <https://github.com/Konstellation-Network/konstellation/security/advisories/new>
- Any other repository: open its **Security** tab and choose **Report a
  vulnerability**.

Do **not** open a public issue, pull request or discussion about a
vulnerability.

Please include:

- the affected repository, version or commit, and network (devnet, testnet or
  mainnet), if any
- the impact, and the steps or a proof of concept to reproduce it

We will acknowledge your report, keep you informed while we fix it, and credit
you in the advisory if you would like to be credited.

## Scope

The node software, genesis and network configuration, preinstalled contracts,
and the other repositories in this organisation. Please do not test against
networks you do not operate, and do not access or change other people's funds
or data.

Details of a bug bounty will be published here before mainnet launches.
